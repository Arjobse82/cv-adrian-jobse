Production automation

I write plugins and scripts for InDesign, Photoshop and Figma that take the repetitive work out of resizing, versioning and catalogue builds. Six are published on Adobe Exchange and Figma Community.

  • InDesign Automated Assemblr Builds catalogue pages from an Excel brief and folders of images, and keeps the pages and the brief in sync both ways.
  • InDesign Assemblr Arranges images inside one frame and lines cut-outs up by their visible edges. Resize the frame and the layout follows.
  • Photoshop Assemblr for Photoshop The same arrangements on Photoshop layers, with every layer left editable.
  • Figma Frame Multiplier Builds every size of a campaign from a handful of master layouts.
  • Figma IDML Export Rebuilds Figma frames as IDML files for InDesign, with CMYK, bleed and paragraph styles.
  • Figma Bloom Preflight for Figma frames: low-res images, off-pixel positions, detached instances and raw colours.

On freelance work I use the agency's own templates and workflow. The tools are there for jobs that suit them. More on AI & Automation
