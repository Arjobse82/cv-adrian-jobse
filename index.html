What I can do for your studio

Send me the approved creative and the media schedule, and I'll send back checked, print-ready and press-ready files in every size on the list. I can work in your studio in Sydney or remotely, by the day or by the job.

  • Finished artCatalogue pages, press, out of home, digital display and social, built from the approved layout to the publisher's or printer's spec.
  • Adaptations and resizingOne master into every size on the schedule, from a 630 × 630 social tile to a 1536 × 384 QMS strip. Copy and price points are checked at the smallest size, where they're most likely to break.
  • VersioningProduct, price and date changes across a set of sizes, kept the same from one version to the next.
  • AmendsClient amends taken through to final, with each round checked against the last mark-up.
  • Prepress and outputColour management, bleed and slug, image resolution, and PDF output to publisher specs. Every file is checked in Acrobat before it goes.
  • Design inside a brandNew layouts and formats designed within an existing brand system and its guidelines.
  • MotionSimple After Effects animation for social and digital sizes.
